## Consensus grammar progression (Schritte, Menschen, Nicos Weg, Linie 1 chapters 1-8)

**A1**
1. Present tense, sein/haben, pronouns, W- and yes/no questions, verb second (V2).
2. Gender, der/ein, kein vs nicht, plural.
3. Possessives.
4. Accusative, möchte/mögen, vowel-change verbs.
5. Separable verbs, am/um/von...bis, time-first word order.
6. Modal verbs with the Satzklammer (verb bracket).
7. Perfekt with haben/sein, war/hatte. Timing varies: Schritte lesson 7/14, Menschen 11-12/24, Nicos 13/18; Nicos teaches war/hatte very early.
8. Imperative.
9. Dative: mit + dative, "Wo?" + dative, gefallen/gehören/passen, dative pronouns.
10. gut-besser, gern-lieber, welch-/dies-, dates, denn, würde/könnte as fixed polite phrases.

**A2**

11. weil/dass/wenn/ob with the verb at the end; deshalb/trotzdem.
12. Perfekt of separable, inseparable and -ieren verbs; Präteritum of modal verbs.
13. Two-way prepositions, stellen/stehen and legen/liegen. Nicos already has these in A1.
14. Reflexive verbs; verbs with prepositions, worauf/darauf.
15. Adjective endings, comparative/superlative. Nicos: A1.
16. Konjunktiv II hätte/wäre/würde/könnte/sollte.
17. Dative plus accusative objects and their order; indirect questions; lassen.
18. Passive present.
19. Relative clauses (nominative/accusative). Sources disagree: Menschen end of A2, Nicos A2 chapter 8, Schritte B1.

**B1**

20. Präteritum of all verbs.
21. obwohl/trotzdem, nachdem + Plusquamperfekt, während/bevor/seit/bis.
22. Relative clauses with dative, prepositions, wo/was.
23. Konjunktiv II for the past.
24. zu + infinitive, um...zu/damit, statt/ohne...zu.
25. Two-part connectors (nicht nur...sondern auch, weder...noch, entweder...oder, zwar...aber, je...desto).
26. Genitive with wegen/trotz/während.
27. Passive with modal verbs and in the past; Futur I.
28. n-declension; adjectives as nouns; participles as adjectives.
29. Indirekte Rede: Nicos only.

## Hours

**Official**
- The Integrationskurs has 600 UE of language (1 UE = 45 min, so 450 hours).
  - The first 300 UE lead "schrittweise zum Niveau A2", the next 300 UE to B1.
  - The exam is the DTZ, scored A2/B1.
- MVHS: one module = 100 UE = 25 weekday mornings over 6 weeks, 229 EUR.
- Goethe estimate (search snippet only; the page returns 403): A1 60-150 h, A2 150-260 h, B1 260-490 h, cumulative.

**Estimate for Hayk (not official):** about 300-400 focused hours from about A1 to B1.

| Average per day | Time to B1 |
|---|---|
| 20 min | about 2.5-3 years |
| 45 min | about 13-17 months |
| 90 min | about 7-9 months |

Plan by hours per unit, not by calendar days: A1 units about 10 h, A2 about 20-25 h, B1 about 40-50 h.

## Draft syllabus (15 units, for discussion)

"RU" marks where Russian helps, "HY" where Armenian does (the agent's teaching judgment).
- Russian has no articles, and its genders often differ from German.
- V2 word order is new for both languages.
- Perfekt vs Präteritum is spoken vs written, not aspect.
- Armenian has no gender, so der/die/das is the main memory load.

**A1** (a diagnostic first may compress these units)

1. **Neighbours and colleagues:** greet, introduce, du/Sie, spell; present tense, questions, V2.
2. **Anmeldung at the Bürgerbüro:** form, clerk, appointment; articles, plural, kein/nicht, possessives, Sie-imperative.
3. **Bakery, supermarket, café:** prices, ordering, labels; accusative (RU: familiar, only the masculine article changes), möchte.
4. **Time and appointments:** arrange or move a meeting; separable verbs, modal verbs + Satzklammer, Perfekt, war/hatte (HY: verb-final feels natural from SOV).
5. **Getting around Munich:** the way, timetables, ticket machine; dative prepositions, "Wo?" + dative (RU: wo/wohin like где/куда).
6. **Doctor, pharmacy, calling in sick:** symptoms, dosage, 112, Krankmeldung; dative pronouns (RU: "mir tut der Kopf weh" works like "у меня болит голова").

**A2**

7. **Flat hunting:** ads, viewing, contract; two-way prepositions (RU: в/на + accusative vs prepositional case), adjective endings, comparison.
8. **Problems in the building:** repair call, note to neighbours; weil/dass/wenn, "Könnten Sie...?" (RU: "Не могли бы вы"), reflexive verbs.
9. **Bank, phone contract, insurance:** lost card, Kündigung; indirect questions, verbs with prepositions (RU warning: they don't transfer), lassen, passive present.
10. **Doctor II and Krankenkasse:** medical history, forms; modal verbs in Präteritum, dative plus accusative objects, "Sie sollten...".
11. **Free time, invitations, trips:** invite, decline, station announcements; deshalb/trotzdem, hätte/wäre, relative clauses (RU: который).

**B1**

12. **Work and colleagues:** shifts, leave, meetings; zu + infinitive, um...zu, genitive, n-declension.
13. **Complaints:** faulty order, landlord repair; obwohl, Plusquamperfekt, passive past, Konjunktiv II past.
14. **Offices, insurance, advice centres:** Widerspruch, damage report; relative clauses with prepositions, passive with modal verbs, two-part connectors, Futur I.
15. **Experiences and opinions:** news emails, easy news; Präteritum, unreal wenn-clauses, je...desto.
