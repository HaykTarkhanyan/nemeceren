# A1 gap audit of Units 0-7 and the fix plan (2026-10-03)

**Status:** findings complete (sections 3-5 from the course audit, section 6 from the Learn German
playlists), videos placed in the lessons, **content fixes not started**. Next: section 7, unit by unit,
ahead of Hayk.

## 1. How the gap was found, and why the checks missed it

Hayk spotted it: u1-01 showed the present-tense table (ich heiße, du heißt ...) but never said the rule
(stem + -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en). u1-02 then said "the endings are the same as always", and u1-04 used
"stem" without defining it. Fixed in the working tree on 2026-10-03.

The checks we had all work on **words and answers**: check-content (format, glossary coverage),
check-taught (every German word in an exercise was shown earlier), and the reviewer briefs (sentences,
keys, giveaways, Russian/Armenian claims). Nothing checked **rules**: is every rule a lesson relies on
stated in words somewhere earlier? And nothing compared the course with the **official A1 inventory**,
only with our own syllabus. A table with no rule passes every check.

## 2. Method

1. Two independent reviewer agents (Units 0-3 and 4-7; 231k and 298k tokens, 10 and 13 min), briefed only
   on rule gaps: rules used but never stated, tables without the rule, grammar tested before taught,
   syllabus promises not kept. Read-only, every finding grepped against all of `content/`.
2. The official Goethe A1 grammar inventory (Start Deutsch 1 *Prüfungsziele / Testbeschreibung*,
   pp. 100-106), turned into the checklist `reference/goethe_A1_grammatik.txt`, checked item by item with a script
   (regexes over all lesson text in course order), then each zero-hit verified by hand.
3. A script listing every Goethe A1 lemma (TSV level A1 + DWDS A1 list, 873 lemmas) that appears nowhere in
   lessons, tests or words, also counting glossary forms (isst counts for essen).

Confirmed fine by both reviewers: every grammar point in the SYLLABUS rows 0-7 is explained in words; all
~50 Russian/Armenian comparisons are correct; chunk pointers are kept; no test needs later grammar except
the nicht item below.
